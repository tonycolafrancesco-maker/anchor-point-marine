Lido Isle, Newport Beach

Boat management on Lido Isle.

Lido Isle mixes private residential docks with community dockage, and vessels here tend to be larger and more systems-heavy than the harbor average.

Request a Consultation

Complexity

More boat means more schedule.

A vessel with a generator, a watermaker, air conditioning, a stabiliser system and a full electronics package does not have one maintenance schedule — it has a dozen, running on different clocks. Some track engine hours, some track the calendar, and they only stay coordinated if something is holding all of them at once.

That is the core of what we do. The systems inventory we build on day one is what makes the rest tractable.

Community dockage

Working within association rules.

Lido Isle dockage sits inside a community association structure, which can mean rules about scheduling, contractors and notice for work on or around the docks. We plan around those constraints as part of scheduling a job.
