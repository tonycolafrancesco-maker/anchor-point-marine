The approach

Know what's happening with your boat without having to ask.

Managing a yacht well is not complicated work, but it is relentless work. Zincs, oil, impellers, bottom growth, batteries, documentation renewals — each one is small, and each one becomes expensive the month it gets missed.

Anchor Point runs that schedule for you. We build a full inventory of your vessel's systems, put every service interval on a calendar driven by both time and engine hours, and coordinate the trades who do the work. You get a written record of what was done, when, by whom, and what it cost.

What we do

Four things, done consistently.

Preventive maintenance

A service schedule built from your vessel's actual systems and tracked against both calendar time and engine hours.

Vendor coordination

We scope the job, schedule the trade, meet them at the boat, and confirm the work was completed properly.

Compliance & documentation

Registration, documentation, and insurance renewal dates tracked ahead of their deadlines rather than after them.

Owner reporting

A monthly report covering what was done, what is scheduled next, and what it cost — with photos of the work.
