Why it compounds here

Newport Harbor is a demanding place to keep a boat.

Newport Harbor is one of the largest recreational harbors on the West Coast, and it is a working saltwater environment. Growth on the running gear, corrosion on underwater metals, sun load on canvas and brightwork, and the usage rate of a Southern California season all accumulate faster than most owners expect.

None of that is unusual. What makes it expensive is letting it accumulate unnoticed until the boat is unavailable the weekend you wanted it.

Scope

What we take off your plate.

  • A complete systems inventory — engines, generator, watermaker, HVAC, electronics, hull and running gear — with serial numbers, engine hours and warranty windows recorded
  • A maintenance schedule generated from that inventory, tracked by both calendar time and engine hours
  • Lead-time alerting so haul-outs, bottom work, engine services and survey deadlines surface before they come due
  • Vendor scheduling, attendance and verification of completed work
  • Registration, documentation and insurance-survey deadline tracking
  • Cost capture against each job, rolled into a single monthly statement

Getting started

The first visit is an inventory, not a sales call.

We walk the vessel, record every major system with its serial number and hours, note current condition, and collect the service records and documentation you already have. That becomes the baseline — from it we build the schedule, identify anything already overdue, and give you a written picture of where the vessel actually stands.

Common questions.

What is the difference between yacht management and calling a mechanic when something breaks?

A mechanic fixes what has already failed. Yacht management holds the whole schedule — every system, every due date, every vendor — so most of the work is planned rather than reactive, and so there is one continuous record of what was done to the vessel.

Do you require us to use your vendors?

No. If you have a mechanic or a yard you already trust, we schedule and coordinate them. Our job is that the work happens on time and is documented, not that it is done by any particular shop.

Do you work on vessels at private residential docks?

Yes. Much of Newport Harbor is residential dockage — Balboa Island, Lido Isle, Linda Isle, Bayshores — and we service vessels at private docks as well as in slips and on moorings.
