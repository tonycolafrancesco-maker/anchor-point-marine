Services

What management actually covers.

Four services that work as one system. The maintenance schedule drives the vendor work, the vendor work produces the record, and the record becomes your monthly report.

01 — Preventive maintenance management

A schedule built from your vessel, not a generic checklist.

We start from what is actually installed on your boat — engines, generator, watermaker, HVAC, electronics, hull and running gear — and build the service schedule from those specific systems.

Intervals are tracked two ways at once: by calendar date and by engine hours, because a boat that ran 300 hours this season needs a different schedule from one that ran 40. Each upcoming item carries lead time, so haul-outs, bottom work, and running-gear service get scheduled before they are due rather than after.

  • Full systems inventory with make, model, serial, and hour readings
  • Service intervals tracked by both calendar time and engine hours
  • Lead-time alerting so scheduled work is booked in advance
  • Condition noted and photographed at each visit

02 — Vendor coordination

Someone meets the trade at the boat.

Most of the friction in yacht ownership is not the repair — it is arranging the repair. Getting a quote, matching a schedule, being at the slip when the technician arrives, and confirming afterwards that the work was actually done to standard.

We take that end to end. We scope the job, arrange the contractor, attend the work where attendance matters, and check the result before the invoice is approved.

  • Job scoped and quoted before work is authorised
  • Scheduling handled directly with the trade
  • Attendance at the vessel for work that warrants it
  • Completion checked and photographed before invoice approval
  • Costs captured against the vessel record as the work closes

03 — Compliance & documentation

Renewal dates tracked ahead of the deadline.

Vessel documentation, registration, and insurance all run on renewal cycles that are easy to lose track of and unpleasant to discover late. We hold those dates alongside the maintenance schedule and raise them before they expire.

  • Registration and vessel documentation renewal dates tracked
  • Insurance renewal and survey deadlines calendared with lead time
  • Service records retained and organised per vessel
  • Documentation assembled for survey, sale, or insurance review

We track deadlines and assemble records. We do not provide legal, insurance, or regulatory advice.[PLACEHOLDER: confirm exact compliance scope and any disclaimer wording required by counsel or insurer]

04 — Transparent owner reporting

A written record, every month.

This is the part we care most about. An owner should never have to send a text asking what happened with their boat.

Every month you receive a report covering the work completed that period, the work scheduled next, the current state of your vessel's systems, and the costs incurred — with photographs of the work as evidence, not just line items.

  • Monthly vessel report covering work done, work upcoming, and spend
  • Photo documentation attached to completed work
  • Running service history retained for the life of the engagement
  • Costs itemised per job rather than bundled into a single figure
